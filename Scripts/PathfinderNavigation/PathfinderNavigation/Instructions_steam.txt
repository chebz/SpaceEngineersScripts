[h1]PathfinderNavigation – Control Script[/h1]

Programmable block script that issues commands to the Pathfinder mod.

[b]Commands[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]start[/b]
[*] [b]start GPS:Name:X:Y:Z:[/b]
[*] [b]start GPS:Name:X:Y:Z: PBName[/b]
[*] [b]stop[/b]
[/list]
